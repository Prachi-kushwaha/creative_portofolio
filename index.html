03 / A little about me

Part researcher.
Part storyteller.
Always observing.

I’m Prachi. I’m drawn to the overlap between how people think, what they feel, and the stories they carry. I move between analytical research and creative expression—looking for the insight that makes an idea feel honest.

This portfolio is my evolving creative practice: a place for brand concepts, poetry, visual experiments, and the questions that lead to them.
