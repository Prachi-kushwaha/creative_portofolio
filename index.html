Self-initiated concept · Brand storytelling

The taste
of being little.

A Paper Boat Aam Panna campaign concept about the summers we remember—not because they were perfect, but because we were little enough to live inside them.

Brand · Paper BoatProduct · Aam PannaFormat · Film + social

An independent, speculative portfolio concept. Not commissioned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Paper Boat.

CREATIVE STUDY / 01
Some summers stay.

“A little sip. A whole summer, remembered.”

01 / The starting point

What if nostalgia
wasn’t a place?

We often remember childhood through tiny sensory details: the sharp taste of raw mango, a paper boat floating in the first rain, the afternoon fan, a voice calling us home.

The campaign takes Aam Panna beyond refreshment and places it inside a familiar emotional moment—summer, seen through the eyes of a child.

Human insight

We don’t miss being young every day.

We miss the small, unplanned moments when time felt generous: one more game, one more sip, one more minute outside.

Brand opportunity

Make the memory tangible.

Use the familiar taste of aam panna as a gentle doorway into a summer memory—without making the product promise to bring childhood back.

“The taste of being little.”

02 / The film

Three scenes.
One summer.

A 30–40 second film, told in warm, tactile fragments. The camera stays close to the small things. No big speech. Just the texture of a day that feels familiar.

SCENE 01 / 00:00–00:10
The afternoon

“Bas paanch minute aur.”

A child sits in the courtyard, making a paper boat from an old notebook page. Sunlight is strong; the ceiling fan hums in the background. Someone calls from inside. The child asks for five more minutes.

SCENE 02 / 00:10–00:25
The little ritual

Aam panna. A paper boat.

A glass of aam panna appears beside the child. The first sip, the sour-sweet expression, the boat placed into a shallow stream of rainwater. Let the sound design carry the memory.

SCENE 03 / 00:25–00:40
The return

Some things stay with us.

Match cut to an adult hand holding Paper Boat Aam Panna near a window on a hot day. A paper boat sits on the desk. The sound of a distant call home lingers.

03 / Script & sound

Let the feeling
do the talking.

Keep the voiceover sparse. The film should feel like a memory someone stumbled into—not an explanation of the product.

Voiceover — draft

VO · Warm, intimate, unhurried

“Garmi ki chhuttiyaan…”

“Jab paanch minute aur mil jaate the.”

“Jab baarish ka paani bhi samandar lagta tha.”

“Aur aam panna… bas aam panna nahi tha.”

“Woh ek poora mausam tha.”

End frame

Paper Boat Aam Panna
The taste of being little.

04 / Campaign world

One thought.
Many little moments.

Build a cohesive campaign around the central thought, with different memory-led executions for social, outdoor, and in-store storytelling.

Film

The taste of being little

A 30–40 second hero film, with sensory close-ups and a gentle transition from childhood to adulthood.

Social series

“Bas paanch minute aur”

Short reels built around childhood summer moments: one more game, one more paper boat, one more sip.

Outdoor / print

Some summers stay.

Minimal visual: a paper boat, a glass of aam panna, and a line that leaves space for the audience’s own memory.

Participation

Tell us your summer.

Invite people to share a tiny childhood summer memory. Curate community stories into a digital “summer scrapbook.”

05 / Art direction

Sun-warmed.
Handmade. Familiar.

Art direction should evoke memory through material and atmosphere—not through a pile of nostalgic props.

Palette

Mango, paper, monsoon green

Warm mango yellow, faded notebook cream, earthy terracotta, and soft leafy green. Slightly imperfect, never overly polished.

Camera

Close to the little details

Macro textures: condensation, a folded paper edge, fingers holding a glass, water moving around the boat. Natural daylight.

Sound

Let the room remember

Fan hum, distant neighbourhood sounds, water, a steel glass on a table. Music enters subtly rather than cueing emotion loudly.

Typography

Handwritten, not childish

Pair a restrained serif headline with handwritten accents. Keep product and brand marks clear in the end frame.

06 / The idea in one line

Some summers
stay on the tongue.

Paper Boat Aam Panna becomes a small sensory reminder of a childhood summer: not a promise to turn back time, but a taste that lets a memory visit.
