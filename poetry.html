01 THE WRITER

Not everything we feel
finds its way into words.

I write about human emotions — the tenderness of love, the ache of heartbreak, the sacrifices of parents, the quiet rebellions of women, and the things people feel but rarely express.

I believe anyone can write about an emotion they have felt, or one they have observed closely enough to understand.

— Prachi

04 MY WRITING

THE WAY I SEE THE WORLD

I don't always
begin with words.
Sometimes, I begin
with noticing.

01

I observe.

People, their silences, their habits, and the emotions they leave behind.

02

I write.

Sometimes a few incomplete lines. Sometimes an entire thought.

03

I refine.

I return to the words until they express what I was trying to feel.
